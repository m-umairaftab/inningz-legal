Delete your Inningz account

  • Effective 26 October 2026
  • App Inningz for Android

You can delete your Inningz account and the data linked to it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

ADelete it in the app

This is the fastest way. It takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Inningz and tap the Settings tab.
  2. In the Account card, tap Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning and confirm. Your account and its data are deleted, and the app returns to the sign-in screen.

BAsk us by email

Use this if you can no longer open the app or you have already removed it.

  1. Send an email to info.inningz@gmail.com from the address linked to your Google account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Inningz account.
  3. We confirm that the request is from you, then delete the account. We finish within 30 days, and usually within a few days.

CWhat happens to your data

DataResult
Name, email address, profile picture link, account IDDeleted
Your profileDeleted
Teams, players and squads you createdDeleted
Matches, innings, every ball and scorecards you ownDeleted
Share codes, and records of matches you joined or scoredDeleted
Backups that held any of the aboveUp to 30 days then overwritten
Copies on other people’s phones, for matches you shared with themNot ours to delete they stay on those phones

We keep nothing else after deletion. We do not hold payment details, because Inningz takes none.

DUsing Inningz as a guest

Guest data is stored only on your phone, so there is nothing of yours on our servers. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

EWant to remove only some data?

You can keep your account and delete a single match or team inside the app. When you are online, deleting it also removes it from our servers.

FYour Google account

Deleting your Inningz account does not touch your Google account. To also remove Inningz’s access to it, open your Google Account, go to Security, then Your connections to third-party apps and services, and remove Inningz.
